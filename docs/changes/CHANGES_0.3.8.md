# AshFall 0.3.8 — Highfen, the capital (versionCode 17)

## New
- **Highfen**, a large round capital at the continent centre (180, -10):
  - stone walls ringed by a moat, with four gates crossing it on bridges;
  - stone avenues meeting at a plaza with wells, an Exchange board and gardens;
  - buildings: Treasury (bank), Emporium (general store), Gilded Spoon (cook house), the player shop and a townhouse for sale;
  - guards at every gate, plus citizens.
  - Data: data/towns/highfen.json, generated by tools/world/place_highfen.py.
- **Player house:**
  - Tap the estate clerk inside: the first tap quotes the price, a second tap within 20 s buys it (5000 marks).
  - It has a hearth, oven, furnace, anvil, workbench, quern and a bank chest. Only the owner can use them; AIs never pick them.
  - Ownership is saved (`house_owned`).
- **The player shop is now only in Highfen.** The old shop buildings in Fenhollow, Cinderholt and Saltmere are now walk-in townhouses.
  - AIs from every town browse the Highfen shop.
  - Old saves: an owned shop moves to Highfen with its owner, till and stock. Stock that doesn't fit the 8 stands, plus a refund of the price of any second owned shop, goes to the Highfen bank. Nothing is lost.
- **Roads:**
  - Every town has a 3-wide stone road to Highfen. Trips between towns go through the capital.
  - Each village has a winding 2-wide dirt road to its nearest town.
  - Roads still route around lakes and cross them on bridges.
  - Roadside fence runs, boulders, bushes and old logs: they block walking, but arrows fly over them.
- **Slime:** level 3, 8 HP, passive. It drops slime goo or a few marks, never bones. Camps sit by every town and village.
- **Highfen town theme:** "Capital city music 1", 56 kbps mono ogg. It uses the same fades as the other towns, and combat still overrides it.
- **Placeholder pixel art**, registered in data/sprites.json and tools/art/aseprite_manifest.json, made by tools/art/placeholders_038.py:
  - tiles: road_stone, road_dirt, moat, moat_edge, capital_wall, capital_front, gate_capital, capital_bldg_wall, capital_bldg_front, house_wall, house_front;
  - objects: road_fence, boulder, bush, old_log;
  - actor: slime; item: slime_goo.

## Changed
- Highfen counts as a city for distance bands, so some camps moved:
  - three goblin camps moved out;
  - the Goblin Warlord moved to just north of Millbrook, and its band minimum dropped from 100 to 60;
  - the coal_2 mine moved.
- Fixed a road-builder rounding bug with cheap bridge costs: no diagonal steps over bridges.
- The moat and other open water don't block line of sight.

## Grok's files (Dropbox /Game) and how they were used
- `highfen_capital.json`:
  - **Invalid JSON** (`".": :`) and not in our schema: rectangular rows, objects that don't exist (fountain, street_lamp, statue...), a moat block the code can't read, and a house and shop without the fields our buildings need.
  - **Rebuilt** as a generator that writes our real town format, keeping the intent: round, moated, pale stone, gates, house at 5000 with every station and a bank chest, and the only player shop.
  - Objects we don't have were replaced with existing ones (well, crates, barrels, sacks, trees).
- `world_capital.json`:
  - Its roads/towns lists were merged into data/world.json.
  - Grok's village pairing `cinderholt-barrowby` is about 370 tiles apart, so villages connect to their nearest town instead: millbrook-fenhollow, barrowby-saltmere, oxley-cinderholt.
  - Its road widths and decoration intent were kept.
- `enemies_slime.json`:
  - Valid JSON but not our schema (attack/strength, `coins`, a spawn block, an inline drop list).
  - Converted to our enemy fields plus a drop table, a camp list and a distance band. `coins` became marks, and a slime_goo item was added.
- The task mentioned a `/Game/capital_update/` folder and `player_shop_note.md`; neither exists. The player-shop rules were taken from CAPITAL_CITY.md.
