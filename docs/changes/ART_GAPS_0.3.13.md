# Art gaps: Fenhollow v1 (0.3.13)

1. **The gates are stone shapes redone in wood.** The gatehouses (fh_gate_back/front/side) keep the stone gate silhouettes, recoloured as timber, so they read as squat towers rather than palisade gates. Their roofs are separate fade layers.
2. **Kell is a placeholder sprite.** The generic quest_giver figure stands outside fh_kell_lodge. Kell has no art of his own.
3. **The doorway spurs are drawn as ground, not tiles.** Spurs from each door to its lane are painted into the ground decal as mud strokes. They cannot be changed in the tile layer.
4. **fh_smithy is too small inside for a furnace and anvil.** The Blacksmith uses a flipped fh_kell_lodge as a forge hall, and fh_smithy is the helmets shop.
5. **fh_house_03's door is not on the bottom row.** The row below its door is drawn as a porch over the ground and is not floor.
6. **Unused designs.** fh_house_06, 07, 09 and 11 did not fit the 74x60 layout around the creek, pools and civic buildings.
7. **No palisade corner or end pieces.** The ring is assembled from per-run sprites, and the straight runs into the side gates meet the curve with connectors.
