- Rename Angling -> Fishing (id migration for saves/AI/personas).
- New Ranged skill: bows + arrows (arrows consumed; arrow tiers; ammo slot), ranged style, accuracy/damage from Ranged.
- Tanning station: cowhide -> leather (fee or skill?).
- New Crafting skill: needle + thread in pack -> craft leather into ranger armour from the pack, no station.
- Ranger armour tiers parallel melee armour; ranger armour boosts ranged; melee armour boosts melee attack.
- Crafting/production window: choose item + quantity before crafting (no auto-craft); applies to all production skills; smithing still at anvil, cooking at range etc.
- (t67u) Level 20 ranger armour is made from wolf pelts. Ranged attacks have no range limit, but need line of sight: they can't pass through walls or buildings, but can fly over small plants, rocks and fences.
- (t69u) Joinery becomes FLETCHING (ranged gear only). Use a knife on a log, anywhere with no workbench, to open the crafting window. Logs become unstrung bows, and stringing one with bowstring makes a usable bow. Logs also become arrow shafts, with higher-tier logs giving more shafts. Shafts plus feathers become headless arrows, and headless arrows plus arrowheads (tiered: bronze, iron, steel, dusk, made by Smithing) become arrows. Everything else Joinery made (planks, rods, buckler, etc.) moves to Crafting. Old Joinery XP and AI personas need migrating.
- (t70u) Add a Crafting Supply store in each city, with a spinning wheel inside. Reeds spin into simple string, used for most crafting and fletching. Flax (a new Foraging crop) spins into superior string, needed for higher-level gear and crafting. The user also reports that Prayer drains far too fast in playtesting; fix this.
