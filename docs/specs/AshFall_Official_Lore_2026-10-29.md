# AshFall Official Lore
**Compiled: 2026-10-29**

## Core Premise
Gates of Hell opened at the northern tip of the continent Anthros, scorching the surrounding land into the Scorched Lands of Ash due to the Ash Demon. Humans are non-magic. The player is human. Ash Demon is the main antagonist for free/base content. Post-Ash Demon content is planned later.

## Continent: Anthros
Includes the main landmass and nearby islands. Kingdom of Ashfen.

### Regions
- **Cinder** (north, around Cinderholt): Rocky, colder, higher elevation, boreal forest. Half consumed by the Scorched Lands of Ash.
- **Lower Ashfen**: Temperate, fertile, very forested. Location of Fenhollow. Near sea level.
- **Central Ashfen**: Higher elevation, temperate, a little more rocky than Lower Ashfen, occasional farms and ranches. Capital Highfen.
- **Mere Lands** (southern): Warmer, tropical rainforest, coastal with sandy beaches. Location of Saltmere.

## Ash Demon
Primordial force of consumption, decay, and entropy. Direct opposite of the God of creation. Neither holds power over the other; one cannot exist without the other. Not an intelligent entity (unlike the Creator). Its only ambition is to spread and devour. Can only be confined/bound, not killed.

Originally sealed by Divine means by the Knights of Old 342 years ago. The seal weakened because the last living descendants of the original holy knights died without an heir (Elijah of Saltmere). Angeleth (defiant angelic being) released it ~1000 years after his defiance, using it as a weapon he can aim but not fully control.

### Infection Mechanics
Infects magical creatures most strongly (slimes, giant rats, giants, trolls, etc.). Infects non-magical beings at a much lesser rate, resistible by Divine blessings. This is why some unprotected humans become Cinder Brutes while many others (including the player) do not. Once fully infected, minds become the will of the Ash Demon (hive mind).

Ash-infected creatures cross the Scorch Line into non-Scorch lands, making the entire northern half of Anthros more dangerous. No Scorch spreading mechanic in-game. Scorch Line is currently frozen/halted by several individuals with minor Divine blessings.

## Divine / Prayer
Humans are non-magic, but those who follow Heaven's call may receive Divine blessings (protection, buffs, or other effects). This explains the Prayer skill. The original seal on the Ash Demon was accomplished through such Divine means.

Endgame: Player is gifted greater Divine blessing matching the power of the original holy knights and unlocks Binding Prayer (requires level 45 Prayer). Holds a target still for 10 seconds (usable on NPCs later). Used to bind the Ash Demon.

## History / Ages
- **1st Age**: Unknown period of balance and peace before Angeleth's defiance.
- **2nd Age**: Began with Angeleth's defiance (~1300 years before game). Constant warfare. Angeleth convinced ~1/3 of the Creator's angelic beings to follow him. They used unholy magic to twist existing creatures (humans into orcs, goblins, trolls, giants) and infused plants with magic essence (e.g., giant rats from rats eating magic mushrooms). Loyal angelic beings fought alongside humanity as generals and champions.
- **3rd Age**: Began when the Knights of Old sealed the Gates of Hell (342 years ago). Period of relative peace, though the world still dealt with leftover magical creatures. Game starts in the 3rd Age. Reopening of the Gates does not mark a change of age. Loyal angelic beings withdrew after the seal and have not been seen since (humanity's faith has dwindled due to comfort and peace).

Angeleth defied the Creator about 1300 years before the game. Magical creatures originated around that time. Roughly 1000 years passed before he released the Ash Demon.

## Gates of Hell
Two large obelisks with a portal/rift that opens between them. Origins surpass the 1st Age and are lost to time. Past the Gates (entering the rift) is a hellscape realm where the player enters to confront and bind the Ash Demon.

## Towns & Villages
### Highfen
Capital of the Kingdom of Ashfen. Circular walled city. Interior holds shops, market, Guild of Knights, and the palace throne at the center. Civilian houses circle the outside of the entire city. Main hub for the response to the Scorched Lands of Ash. Design target for next update.

King Hudson the 3rd: Kind and just ruler. Married to Queen Jessica Julie Christine. Four children: Princess Avery and Princess Sophia (twins, ~14), Princess Kylie (~3–5), and newborn Prince Elijah (named after Elijah of Saltmere). Scorch situation has his utmost attention.

### Cinderholt
Industrial capital closest to the Scorch. Known for heavy smithing, arms making, and the best blacksmiths in all of Anthros. Frontline town for containment against the Ash Demon's influence.

### Fenhollow
Peaceful trading, hunting, and farming town with a strong forestry and woodworking guild. Relatively far from the Scorch. Starting town for players and location where the Goblin Slayer quest begins.

### Saltmere
Southernmost coastal town on Anthros, furthest from the Scorch. Features a port for trade and fishing.

### Millbrook
Village closer to the Scorch than Highfen but not as close as Cinderholt. Associated with the Goblin Warlord in the Goblin Slayer quest chain.

### Barrowby
Southernmost village on Anthros. Located north of Saltmere and south of Highfen.

### Oxley
Village east of Highfen, roughly the same distance from the Scorch as the capital. Quiet rural settlement.

### Cindervill
Small village that once stood north of Cinderholt. Now charred ruins inside the Scorched Lands of Ash, surrounded by Cinder Brutes. It was once a mining hub that sent ores down into Cinderholt for the blacksmiths. With it gone and the Scorch threat present, the king redirected all mines to send ores north to Cinderholt, but the region remains short of supply. Also the source of the werewolf war pack Elijah defended against in his early 20s.

## Churches & Priests
Churches are in need of repair. Priests are always excited to greet newcomers and may mention the low attendance they have experienced. As the conflict grows, more people begin attending and faith starts to return.

Head priests of churches across Ashfen are currently absent. They are the individuals with minor Divine blessings who are holding the Scorch Line in place. In their absence, a devoted lay member has stepped up to fill each priest's role at the churches.

These head priests are gathered at the Northern Guard camp. They use their Prayer skill and Divine blessings to keep the Scorch from spreading further.

## Key NPCs & Figures
### Elijah of Saltmere
Last living descendant of the original Knights of Old. Carried the last remnant of the original Divine blessing that once strengthened the seal. Lived quietly in Saltmere in later years, training the local guard in practical combat and remnants of old knightly ways. His first wife was murdered by a demonic necromancer (ties to the hellish realm beyond the Gates). The necromancer had previously tried to open the Gates of Hell; Elijah slew him and stopped the attempt, saving the kingdom from greater evil. He never remarried and had no children — ending the direct bloodline. Died of old age one week before the Gates reopened. His death is seen by those who remember the old stories as the final weakening of the ancient Divine seal. Tomb lies just outside Saltmere (reserved for a later quest). The newborn prince was named after him. Highly respected among nobles and royals. Those old enough to remember his glory days still see him as a hero. In his early 20s he slayed a vampire that tormented Cinderholt and defended against a werewolf war pack from Cindervill. Most common folk knew him as a respected old trainer; the Guild of Knights (especially Talius) knew he was the last true heir.

### Guild of Knights
Vague leftover of the Knights of Old. Only 6 members, none of the original bloodline. Leader is Knight Talius, close friend of Elijah who earned knighthood through adventures with the last true heir. He mourns Elijah and seeks to uphold his name and the guild. Talius knows the location of the old ruined temple (Lost Knowledge quest). He has never passed the first puzzle, so the Guild of Knights still does not know what was sealed (the Ash Demon).

### Player Origin & Intro
Traveler from Vellenheir, a continent west of Anthros. Ship wrecks on a small island near Fenhollow; player wakes as the only survivor alongside an NPC named Gilbert who gives the semi-comedic tutorials. Completes a tutorial (movement, combat, resource gathering, fishing, cooking, mining, woodcutting, crafting) then builds a raft to reach the mainland near Fenhollow as an unknown outsider. On arrival Gilbert tells the player to travel east to Fenhollow to start their adventure for fame and fortune. This concludes the tutorial session and intro to the game. No Divine blessing at start.

Starting shore: New starting point after the tutorial. Small abandoned dock on the beach where the raft sits (may be used for travel to other islands later). Direct dirt path leads east to Fenhollow. Along the path are low-level 1-3 NPCs: slimes and the weakest variant of giant rats. Gilbert stays at the dock after the tutorial and directs the player toward Fenhollow. The game is open world, but the path is linear and blocked by cliffs to force the player toward Fenhollow.

Dialog rule: Do not refer to the player as the hero or chosen one. That is not known until much later in the main quest. NPCs stay practical.

Knowledge rule: Do not reference the Ash Demon by name until the Lost Knowledge quest is completed. Until then, NPCs and text only speak of something evil sealed by the knights of old behind the Gates of Hell.

## Creatures & Monsters
### Goblins
Small messy clusters (3–6), defective stake fences, tents, sacks/crates, central fire pit. Goblins wander short radius, aggro on approach, chase moderate distance. Night: stay nearer fire. Type 2 spawn (less common): Raid on a trader's cart. Includes a dead human corpse, broken-tired overturned wagon, and spilled boxes/supply sacks. Ash Goblins use dusty camp props, more aggressive, appear in northern half.

### Slimes
Pest creatures found in swampy water areas and outside towns. Low-level, amorphous, passive, drop no bones. Ash variants appear closer to the Scorch.

### Wolves
Aggressive pack hunters found in wilderness areas. Ash Wolves / Cinder Wolves are corrupted variants that appear in the northern half and near the Scorch. Dire Wolves are magic-infused (bigger and stronger than normal wolves).

### Cinder Brutes
Once human men who lacked Divine blessing/protection. Corrupted by the Ash Demon's influence into hulking ash-touched brutes. Typical spawn: burnt-down cottage (their original home). They linger close to where they were turned, but sometimes range further south.

### Giant Rats
Aggressive oversized rodents found in wilderness and near settlements. Common low-level threat; ash-corrupted variants appear in the northern half. Typically include a decorative rat burrow; ground textures lean toward mud and dirt in the spawn area. Magic Mushrooms (cyan glowing) can be foraged; tied to giant rat size in lore.

### Giants
Magical creatures that originated the same way as other magical beings (shaped by Angeleth). They can be corrupted by the ash into ash variants. They tend to stay away from humans and dwell in dark, deep caves. Around level 25. Ash Giant variants: around level 50. Drop giant bones. Burying giant bones grants about 25 Prayer XP. Spawns: Inside or outside their caves. Typically include a large giant bonfire (usable for cooking). Giants have large animal pelts as bedding and clothing, and carry large tree-branch clubs.

### Lesser Ash Demons / Gate Spawns
Level 40 manifestations of the Ash Demon's influence. Emerge near or from the Gates of Hell; direct embodiments of its ruinous force rather than corrupted living beings.

### Ash Wraiths
Incorporeal ash spirits formed from the Demon's influence. Harder to hit, appear in heavily corrupted zones near the Scorch and Gates.

### Ash Demon Champion / Gate Guardian
Level 50 (high-tier, above Level 40 Gate Spawns and mid-tier Level 30 guards).

### Soldiers
New human NPC type. Level 35 combat (vs level 30 guards). Visibly more armored than typical guards. Found at the Northern Guard camp and similar posts.

### Vampires
Exist as NPCs in the world. Will be used for a specific quest to slay one.

## Skills & Resources
### Woodcutting Tiers
Tier 1 — Normal/Dead trees + Pine (all regions). Tier 2 — Oak (Lower & Central Ashfen). Tier 3 — Ash (Lower Ashfen). Tier 4 — Spruce (Cinder). Tier 5 — Mahogany (Mere Lands). One primary tree type per tier after Tier 1.

### Ore Distribution
Copper and tin mostly in Lower Ashfen. Iron and dusk ore in the Cinder region. Coal abundant in the Mere Lands. Central Ashfen has some copper, tin, and iron. Dusk ore: Used in place of iron ore to create dusk steel, which is one tier stronger than regular steel. Found mainly in the Cinder region.

### Skill Rework
Joinery is replaced by the Crafting skill (includes woodworking and other crafting). New Fletching skill added for arrow making and bow making.

## Quests
### Goblin Slayer Chain (Influence gating)
**Part 1 — Goblin Slayer** (already implemented): Starts in Fenhollow. Kell sends you to village elder who then sends you to the goblin warlord. Features only normal goblins. +10 Influence.

**Part 2 — The Ash Touch**: Unlocks at 15 Influence (Goblin Slayer gives +10, so a side quest is needed). Objective is kill Ash Goblins until they drop Ash Goblin Remains (quest-only drop). Introduces Ash Goblins. Shows spread of Ash Demon's influence.

**Part 3 — Warlord of Ash**: Unlocks at 25 Influence. A new Goblin Warlord came from the north bringing Ash Goblins with him.

### Side Quests (+5 Influence each)
**Night of 1000 Howls** (formerly Wolf Pelts, Fenhollow): First part is killing wolves and retrieving 5 pelts. Once done, the player is notified about a Dire Wolf (magic-infused, bigger and stronger than normal wolves) that is drawing packs of wolves near the farms and villages, and tasked to slay it.

**A Dead Rat is a Good Rat** (Millbrook): Kill 6 rats, return, then kill the Rat Mother in her den (slightly larger and stronger rat). Quest giver is old and oblivious to world events; provides no real lore about the Ash Demon, Scorch, or northern situation.

**Chicken Slayer**: Gather 10 raw chicken and 30 feathers for food and arrows to send north to the cause.

### Other Quests
**We Need Steel!** (Cinderholt blacksmith): Collect 5 iron and 10 coal (mining recommended or buy). Deliver to Northern Guard camp. Rewards: good smithing XP, gold, and +5 Influence.

**Man of Science!**: Quest giver is a scientist. Investigate how ash infects magical creatures so easily. Gather samples from 3 different infected variants, return results, then deliver to the King of Highfen.

**Lost Knowledge**: Requires 50 Influence to start (follows Man of Science). Ruined temple, 2 puzzles, Lesser Ash Demon (fight or run past), ends with tablet showing the knights of old sealing the evil. Reveals the "what" but not the "how".

**Can We Fix It?**: Farm near Cinderholt attacked by creatures moving south from the Scorched Lands of Ash. Player supplies wood planks to repair broken fences and damaged walls.

**Finding Clip Clop**: Horse named Clip Clop is found near a wolf den. Player must fight off the wolves to save it, then return the horse.

### Main Questline
Starts in Highfen at the Guild of Knights (requires enough influence to join). After completing the first quest of the line and proving yourself, the player receives a greater Divine blessing matching the power of the original holy knights. Ends with the player binding the Ash Demon (cannot be killed). Player enters the hellscape realm past the Gates of Hell.

## Other Lore
Ash variants of non-magical creatures exist but are rare and only found in the Scorched Lands of Ash (e.g., Ash Wolves). Magic-infused creatures such as Dire Wolves are corrupted by ash more commonly than their normal counterparts.

First boss: Ash Goblin King. Unlocked after Warlord of Ash. His main camp was in northern Cinder (now Scorched Lands of Ash). He has been taken by the ash and leads hordes of ash-affected goblins south. Player must defeat him.

Orc quest line (after Ash Goblin King): 1) Help a nearby village defeat orc raiders. Suspicion they are linked to the Gates (they are not). 2) Enter an orc underground village to collect evidence — reveals orcs do not know what is happening either and are only taking advantage. 3) Stop the raids. 4) Defeat 2nd boss: the orc chief's pet troll, then confront the orc chief. Ends with the orc chief dead. Orc NPCs still roam the land afterward and can be fought as normal monsters.

Timeline: Elijah of Saltmere dies. 1 week later the Gates of Hell open. 2 weeks after the Gates open the player arrives. Tomb is recent.
