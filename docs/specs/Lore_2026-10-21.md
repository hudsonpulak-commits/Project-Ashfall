# AshFall Lore Briefing
**Document Date:** 2026-10-21  
**Project:** AshFall (RuneScape-inspired top-down pixel art RPG)

## Setting Overview
- **Continent:** Anthros (includes the main landmass and all nearby islands).
- **Kingdom:** Kingdom of Ashfen.
- **Current Age:** 3rd Age. The game begins in the 3rd Age. The reopening of the Gates of Hell does **not** mark a change of age.
- **Timeline of Current Crisis:**
  - Elijah of Saltmere dies of old age.
  - 1 week later: The Gates of Hell open.
  - 2 weeks after the Gates open: The player enters the world.
  - Total: Player arrives ≈3 weeks after Elijah’s death and ≈2 weeks after the Gates reopened.

## Cosmology & Divine Faith
- Humans follow the **God of all things good** — the entity that brought reality into existence. This God created all non-magical beings, the lands, and the heavens. Assisted by angelic beings who carry out the Divine will.
- **Angeleth:** The angelic being who defied the Creator approximately 1300 years ago. He could not create as the Creator could, but he could shape and mold existing creatures with magic. This is the origin of all magical beings (goblins, orcs, giants, trolls, etc.). Magic is regarded as the opposite of Divine blessing; people of faith view magic as evil. Structure mirrors a classic God/adversary framework.
- **Divine blessing** is gifted to those found worthy. Humans cannot use magic themselves but can interact with it through potion brewing.
- **Ash Demon:** Has always existed alongside the God of creation as a direct opposite. It is the essence of consumption and decay (not an intelligent entity). Neither holds power over the other; one cannot exist without the other. Where the God brings new things and life, the Ash Demon decays and devours life. This is why the Ash Demon can only be confined, never killed.
- Angeleth uses the Ash Demon as a weapon. He is the intelligent entity who directs it. He does not have full control, but he can aim the Ash Demon at targets for it to consume. He originally released it onto the old world.

## Historical Ages
- **1st Age:** An unknown period of balance and peace before Angeleth’s defiance.
- **2nd Age:** Began with Angeleth’s defiance (~1300 years ago). An age of constant warfare. Angeleth convinced about one-third of the Creator’s angelic beings to follow him. They used unholy magic to twist existing creatures (turning humans into orcs, goblins, trolls, and giants) and infused plants with magic essence. Normal creatures that ate the plants were altered (e.g., giant rats from rats eating magic mushrooms). Loyal angelic beings fought alongside humanity as generals and champions.
- **Sealing:** The Knights of Old sealed the Gates of Hell 342 years ago, beginning the 3rd Age.
- **3rd Age:** Relative peace, though the world still dealt with leftover magical creatures and effects from Angeleth’s defiance. Loyal angelic beings withdrew after the sealing and have not been seen since. Humanity’s faith was strong then; in the present it has dwindled due to comfort and peace, which is why the angelic beings do not appear during the current conflict.

## The Current Crisis
- The **Gates of Hell** (northern tip of Anthros) reopened. They consist of two large obelisks with a portal/rift that opens between them. Their origins predate the 1st Age and are lost to time.
- The surrounding area is now called the **Scorched Lands of Ash**.
- **Public knowledge at game start:** Only that something evil the knights of old sealed away has broken free. The name “Ash Demon” is **not known** until the Lost Knowledge quest is completed. Until then, refer only to “something evil” sealed by the knights of old behind the legendary Gates of Hell.
- The ash infects magical creatures the most (slimes, giant rats, giants, trolls, goblins, etc.). It also infects non-magical beings at a much lesser rate. Divine blessings make the infection resistible. This is why some unprotected humans become Cinder Brutes while many others (including the player) do not.
- Once fully infected, creatures’ and humans’ minds become the will of the Ash Demon, functioning like a hive mind.
- The Ash Demon’s only ambition is to spread and devour.
- The Scorch Line is currently frozen/halted (no spreading mechanic). Several individuals with minor Divine blessings have stopped further spread. Ash-infected creatures still cross into the northern half of the kingdom, making it more dangerous.
- Past the Gates lies a hellscape realm. This is where the player enters to confront and bind the Ash Demon at the end of the main quest. The Ash Demon is the main antagonist for the free/base content.

## Key Historical & Present Figures
- **Knights of Old:** A religious order of Divine knights. Humans slowly strayed from the faith during a long period of peace, weakening Divine protection. Combined with the end of the bloodline, this allowed the sealed evil to emerge.
- **Elijah of Saltmere:** Last living descendant of the original knights. Trained the local guard in Saltmere before dying of old age. Had no wife or children, ending the lineage. His tomb is located outside Saltmere (recent; reserved for a later quest).
- **King Hudson the 3rd:** Kind and just ruler. The Scorched Lands of Ash situation has his utmost attention; he worries for the safety of his subjects and kingdom. Ordered the formation of the Northern Guard and mass production of weapons/armor from Cinderholt. Directed all mines to send ores north to Cinderholt.
- **Guild of Knights:** Vague leftover of the Knights of Old. Only 6 members, none of the original bloodline. Leader is **Knight Talius**, close friend of Elijah who earned knighthood through adventures with the last true heir. He mourns Elijah and seeks to uphold his name and the guild. Talius knows the location of the old ruined temple but has never passed the first puzzle, so the Guild still does not know what was sealed.
- **Northern Guard:** Consists of guards, soldiers, and volunteers from all towns and villages. Formed by direct order of King Hudson the 3rd to face the Scorch threat. Camp just north of Cinderholt: human-style tents, supply wagons (weapons/ammo, decorative), spiked defensive fences and barriers, torches, central campfire. Occupied by guards (level 30) and soldiers (level 35, visibly more armored).

## Regions of the Kingdom of Ashfen
- **Cinder** (north, around Cinderholt): Rocky, colder, higher elevation, boreal forest. Half consumed by the Scorched Lands of Ash. Closest town to the crisis. Industrial capital known for heavy smithing, arms making, and the best blacksmiths in all of Anthros. A messenger from Cinderholt first brought news to Highfen. Cinderholt guards were the first to patrol and hold ash entities back. Northern Guard camp is just north of Cinderholt.
- **Lower Ashfen** (named for lower elevation near sea level): Temperate, fertile, and very forested. Farming and forestry focus. Fenhollow (peaceful starting town with strong forestry and woodworking guild; location of Goblin Slayer start).
- **Central Ashfen** (higher elevation, central point of the kingdom): Temperate and a little more rocky than Lower Ashfen; occasional farms and ranches. Capital Highfen.
- **Mere Lands** (southern): Warmer and more tropical rainforest climate, coastal with sandy beaches. Saltmere (southernmost coastal town with a port for trade and fishing; furthest from the Scorch).

**Highfen (Capital):** Circular walled city. Interior holds shops, market, Guild of Knights, and the palace throne at the center. Civilian houses circle the outside of the entire city. Main hub for the response to the Scorched Lands of Ash. (Design target for next update.)

## Resources & Skills
- **Ore Distribution:**
  - Lower Ashfen: mostly copper and tin.
  - Cinder: iron and dusk ore.
  - Mere Lands: abundant coal.
  - Central Ashfen: some copper, tin, and iron.
- **Dusk Ore:** Used in place of iron ore to create dusk steel (one tier stronger than regular steel). Found mainly in Cinder.
- **Woodcutting Tiers** (one primary tree type per tier after Tier 1):
  - Tier 1 (level 1): Normal/Dead trees + Pine (all regions / Lower Ashfen).
  - Tier 2 (level 15): Oak (Lower & Central Ashfen).
  - Tier 3 (level 30): Ash (Lower Ashfen).
  - Tier 4 (level 45): Spruce (Cinder).
  - Tier 5 (level 60): Mahogany (Mere Lands).
- **Skill Rework:** Joinery is replaced by the **Crafting** skill (includes woodworking and other crafting). New **Fletching** skill added for arrow making and bow making.
- **New Foraging Item:** Magic Mushrooms — cyan glowing mushrooms. Can be foraged; tied to giant rat size in lore.

## Creatures & Spawns
- Magical creatures originated via Angeleth and are highly susceptible to ash corruption. Non-magical beings resist better (especially with Divine blessings).
- **Goblins:** Usually spawn in small messy camps (3–6): goblin tents, defective wooden stake fences, sacks and crates of supplies, central fire pit (can cook over). Wander short radius, aggro on approach, chase moderate distance. Night: stay nearer fire.  
  - Type 2 spawn (less common): Raid on a trader’s cart — dead human corpse, broken-tired overturned wagon, spilled boxes/supply sacks.
  - Ash Goblins: Dusty camp props, more aggressive, appear in northern half.
- **Giant Rats:** Aggressive oversized rodents. Spawns centered on decorative rat burrow; ground textures lean toward mud and dirt. 2–5 rats, loose patrol, closer aggro than wolves. Ash Rats: ashen ground, larger aggro. Larger than normal because they eat magic mushrooms.
- **Wolves:** Aggressive pack hunters in clearings/forest edges (wide roam, longer aggro). Ash Wolves in northern transition zones.
- **Slimes:** Pests found in swampy water areas and outside towns. Low-level, amorphous, passive, drop no bones. Ash variants closer to the Scorch.
- **Cinder Brutes:** Once human men who lacked Divine blessing/protection. Corrupted into hulking ash-touched brutes. Typical spawn: burnt-down cottage (their original home). Linger close to where they were turned, but sometimes range further south.
- **Giants:** Magical creatures. Level ~25 (Ash Giant variants ~50). Tend to stay away from humans and dwell in dark, deep caves. Spawns inside or outside caves: large giant bonfire (usable for cooking), large animal pelts as bedding/clothing, carry large tree-branch clubs. Drop giant bones (≈25 Prayer XP when buried).
- **Orcs:** Strong warrior race infused with magic. Main threat to mankind since the 2nd Age. Dormant and peaceful toward humans since before the Gates were sealed. Re-emerged from underground towns/villages after the Gates reopened to raid humans while the kingdom is distracted. After their quest line ends (orc chief dead), orc NPCs still roam and can be fought as normal monsters.
- **Lesser Ash Demons / Gate Spawns:** Level 40 manifestations. Emerge near or from the Gates; direct embodiments of its force.
- **Ash Wraiths:** Incorporeal ash spirits. Harder to hit, appear in heavily corrupted zones.
- **Ash Demon Champion / Gate Guardian:** Level 50.

## Quests & Influence System
Influence points unlock higher quests and the Guild of Knights. Completing side quests after Goblin Slayer reaches thresholds.

**Goblin Slayer Chain (shows spread of influence):**
- Part 1: Goblin Slayer (starts in Fenhollow with Kell). Kill 5 goblins → village elder in Millbrook → kill Goblin Warlord (level 12). +10 Influence.
- Part 2: The Ash Touch (15 Influence). Kill Ash Goblins until Ash Goblin Remains (quest-only drop).
- Part 3: Warlord of Ash (25 Influence). New Goblin Warlord from the north bringing Ash Goblins.

**First Boss:** Ash Goblin King (after Warlord of Ash). Original main camp in northern Cinder (now Scorched Lands). Taken by the ash; leads hordes of ash-affected goblins south. Player must defeat him.

**Orc Quest Line (after Ash Goblin King):**
- Help a nearby village defeat orc raiders. Suspicion they are linked to the Gates (they are not).
- Enter an orc underground village to collect evidence — reveals orcs do not know what is happening either and are only taking advantage of the distraction.
- Stop the raids.
- Defeat 2nd boss: the orc chief’s pet troll, then confront the orc chief. Line ends with chief dead; orcs remain as normal monsters.

**Side Quests (+5 Influence each unless noted):**
- Wolf Pelts (Fenhollow): Hunter tells of farm terrorized by wolves; return with 5 pelts.
- A Dead Rat is a Good Rat (Millbrook): Old oblivious villager. Kill 6 rats, return, then kill Rat Mother. Rats large from magic mushrooms. No real lore from giver.
- Chicken Slayer: Gather 10 raw chicken + 30 feathers for food and arrows to send north.
- We Need Steel! (Cinderholt blacksmith): Collect 5 iron + 10 coal (mine or buy). Deliver finished goods to Northern Guard camp. Rewards: good smithing XP, gold, +5 Influence. Notes supply shortages despite redirected mines.
- Can We Fix It?: Farm near Cinderholt attacked by creatures from the Scorched Lands. Supply wood planks to repair broken fences and damaged walls.
- Finding Clip Clop: Horse named Clip Clop found near a wolf den. Fight off wolves to save it, then return the horse.

**Mid/Late Quests:**
- Man of Science! (30 Influence): Scientist. Gather samples from 3 different infected variants, return results, deliver to King of Highfen.
- Lost Knowledge (50 Influence, follows Man of Science): Ruined temple, 2 puzzles, Lesser Ash Demon (fight or run past). Ends with tablet depicting the knights of old sealing the evil. Reveals the “what” but not the “how.” Do not name the Ash Demon before this quest.
- Main quest starts in Highfen at the Guild of Knights (enough influence to join). After proving yourself on the first quest of the line, receive greater Divine blessing matching the power of the holy knights of old.
- Endgame: Player gifted greater Divine blessing and unlocks Binding Prayer in Prayer skill (requires level 45 Prayer). Holds a target still for 10 seconds (usable on NPCs later). Used to bind the Ash Demon (cannot be killed) in the hellscape past the Gates.

**Churches:** In need of repair. Priests are always excited to greet newcomers and may mention low attendance. As the conflict grows, more people attend and faith starts to return.

**Dialog Rules:**
- Do not refer to the player as the hero or chosen one. That is not known until much later.
- NPCs stay practical. Kell and most early NPCs have limited knowledge.
- No direct “Ash Demon” name until Lost Knowledge is completed.

## Endgame & Post-Content
- Player enters the rift/hellscape past the Gates to confront and bind the Ash Demon.
- Binding Prayer (level 45) is the key tool.
- Ash Demon is the main antagonist for free/base content. Post-Ash Demon content is planned for later.
