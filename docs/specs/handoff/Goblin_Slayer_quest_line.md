# ASHFEN — Goblin Slayer quest line
Handoff for Lead Game Developer. Second quest line. Cap is 100; tier 4 at level 30 is mid-game. This line is the first trip out of Fenhollow and ends on a named goblin, not a dungeon.

## Who gives it
Sergeant Kell, Fenhollow north gate. Not Old Wren. Kell has no marker until the tutorial is finished and influence is at least 10 (Scorch-approach threshold, already reached by completing Wren).

Kell gives every step. Other cities have a contact you must talk to, then you return to Kell, except the warlord step, which is turned in at Kell after the kill. One active quest. Same pack rule as the tutorial: if the reward items will not fit, Kell says "Come back when you have space in your pack to receive the reward." Coins and influence wait with the items. Next step is offered only after the reward fits.

Each step pays 2 influence. Five steps, 10 influence. A character who finished the tutorial (10) ends this line at 20, which is the cave threshold for later. This line does not require the cave.

AI ignore the influence gate. Goblins and the warlord exist whether or not the player is on the quest. The quest only marks them and pays the reward.

## The camp
Goblin camp, not a city. South-east of the Fenhollow–Cinderholt road, about 80 tiles from Fenhollow, outside every town wall. Single combat. 8 goblin huts, a fire, a banner. Respawning goblins around the huts. The warlord does not respawn while the quest is active; after turn-in he respawns on a 10 minute timer like other named camps later.

Regular goblin: combat level 10. Warlord: combat level 32, named Garr the Red. He does not aggro until attacked or until a hut goblin is in combat with the player inside his 8-tile radius. Leash 14 from his banner. Overhead level uses the nameplate colors already specced.

## The line

### 1. Missing patrol (2 influence)
Needs 10 influence, tutorial complete.
- Kell's gate pair never came back from the Cinderholt road.
- Walk the road marker to the broken cart (40 tiles toward Cinderholt). Loot the patrol token off the ground. Three goblins, combat 10, may be there. They are optional.
- Bring the token to Kell.
- Reward: 150 marks, 2 influence, 20 cooked beef, a bronze sword if they have no weapon (otherwise 10 bones).

### 2. Cinderholt report (2 influence)
Teaches the other forge city.
- Take Kell's sealed note to Captain Hest at the Cinderholt north gate.
- Hest answers only if the note is in the pack. He gives a basalt tally (quest item) and one line: the goblins are buying steel scrap from his dumps.
- Return the tally to Kell.
- Reward: 200 marks, 2 influence, an iron helm (level 10). If defence is under 10, it stays in the pack until they can wear it.

### 3. Saltmere steel (2 influence)
Teaches the harbour city.
- Kell sends you to the Saltmere blacksmith, Bryn. She wants 5 steel bars as proof the scrap is real, or 200 marks if steel is not smithed yet. Either satisfies the step. Do not force a level-20 smithing requirement on a quest that starts at influence 10.
- Bryn marks a camp bearing on the map.
- Return to Kell.
- Reward: 200 marks, 2 influence, 50 iron arrows (Fenhollow archery goods; vendor trash until ranged is in).

### 4. The red banner (2 influence)
Opens the camp.
- Reach the goblin camp marker from Bryn's bearing.
- Kill 8 goblins there. Cuts do not count from the road ambush in step 1.
- Pull the banner down (one interact at the fire).
- Reward, paid by Kell on return: 250 marks, 2 influence, an iron longsword (level 10 attack). Same pack-until-you-can-wear rule.

### 5. Garr the Red (2 influence)
The warlord.
- Kell tells you the banner was Garr's. Return to the camp and kill Garr the Red (combat 32).
- He drops Garr's blade always: an iron sword with a better slash than the shop iron longsword, still level 10 attack, not tradable until the quest is turned in. After turn-in it becomes a normal tradeable weapon so the economy gets one extra iron sword, not a unique sink forever.
- Also drops, always: 400 marks, 2 coal, 1 steel bar.
- Rare extra, 1 in 8 after the quest is turned in and he is on his timer: a second steel bar. Not on the quest kill, so the quest kill is not the farm.
- Bring Garr's blade to Kell. He looks at it and gives it back. That is the turn-in, not a confiscation.
- Reward: 400 marks, 2 influence, a steel med-helm (level 20). Sits in the pack if defence is under 20.

## Totals
Influence: 10, taking a tutorial finisher from 10 to 20.
Coins: 1,200 from Kell, plus 400 on the warlord, 1,600 total.
Gear: iron helm, iron longsword, steel med-helm, Garr's blade. First iron the quest line guarantees. Steel helm is the stretch reward, not required to finish.

## Combat notes
Garr max hit should be survivable in iron with the food from step 1, not in an empty bronze pack. If the player is under combat 20, Kell says the camp is ahead of them but does not block the step. Overhead level will show red.

Death on the road is the normal rule: keep items, 10% coins. The camp is not Scorch and not multi-combat. Garr's blade is a quest item until turn-in: kept on death, no sell, no drop.

## Not in this pass
No goblin dungeon. No second warlord. Cave content stays behind influence 20, which this line reaches but does not enter.
