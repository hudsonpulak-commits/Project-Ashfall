# ASHFEN — Item XP by skill
Handoff for Lead Game Developer. XP is per item action (bury, cook, smelt, smith one piece, fletch one, tan one). Cap is 100. Tier 4 is mid-game. All values in JSON.

Prayer bones use the numbers already called: normal bones 5, wolf bones 7, giant bones 20.

## Prayer (bury)
| Item | XP |
|---|---|
| Bones (rat, chicken, goblin, cow) | 5 |
| Wolf bone | 7 |
| Giant bone | 20 |
| Dire wolf bone (add on dire wolves, if they drop a bone) | 12 |

Altar refill gives no XP. It only restores points.

## Smithing
Smelt XP is per bar. Smith XP is per finished item (weapon, armour piece, arrowhead batch of 15 counts as one smith action at the batch XP).

| Item | Level | XP |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze bar | 1 | 8 |
| Iron bar | 12 | 18 |
| Steel bar | 20 | 28 |
| Dusk bar | 30 | 42 |
| Bronze item (dagger, sword, helm, body, legs, shield, arrowheads) | 1 | 12 |
| Iron item | 10–15 | 28 |
| Steel item | 20–25 | 45 |
| Dusk item | 30 | 70 |

A body can use the same XP as a dagger at that tier. Do not pay more XP for a body or smiths will only make bodies.

## Combat skills
No item gives Attack, Strength, Defence, Ranged, or Vitality XP. Those come from landing hits. Food and armour do not grant combat XP.

## Not XP
Coins, quest items, Garr's blade, Greymaw's quest hide, arrowheads as a loose stack (XP was already paid when smithed), bars sitting in the pack.
