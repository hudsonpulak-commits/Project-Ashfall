# ASHFEN — Giant cave
Handoff for Lead Game Developer. First cave. Opens at 20 influence, which either Kell's line or Pye's line reaches alone.

## Where
One cave mouth on the iron-mine road, about 68 tiles from the nearest city, same band as the iron rocks. Not in the Scorch. Not under the goblin camp. Single combat in the entrance tunnel. The giant chamber at the back is multi-combat, the only multi room outside the Scorch.

## Giants
Combat level 30. Passive until attacked. Leash 12 from the tile they spawned on. Two in the side pockets, three in the chamber. They do not leave the cave.

Overhead level uses the existing nameplate colors. A fresh tutorial account will see red. Kell and Pye do not send you here.

## Drops
Each giant, every kill:
- 1 giant bone. Bury anywhere for 20 Prayer XP. Not a normal bone. Rat and wolf bones stay on their own smaller XP value.
- 1 in 3: 1 iron ore.
- 1 in 8: 1 coal.
- 1 in 16: 1 steel bar.

No dire pelt. No quest item. Giant bones can be banked. They are not quest-items, so they drop on a Scorch death like any other item. The cave is not the Scorch, so a death here is the normal keep-items, 10% coin rule.

## Not in this pass
No giant boss. The warlord and Greymaw stay the named fights.
