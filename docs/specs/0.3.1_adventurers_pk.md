# 0.3.1 spec: Adventurer AI + northern PK zone (user request 2026-10-08 t30u)
User verbatim: "I want adventure ai players that are more like the player and are combat and gear gaining and getting the best stats. Like a real player would. We will add a player killing area that coveres the north end of the map, it's biom changed to chard earth in that area. Some ai go there to player kill to gain other gear and money"

## Adventurer AI (new persona class, ~12 new agents, on top of the 40)
- Visible, player-like: train Attack/Strength/Defence/Vitality/Prayer, use the same combat formulas, eat, pray, bank.
- Gear progression: track best-in-slot by stats & level reqs; buy upgrades from shops/Exchange/player shop, or commission smiths; equip as soon as reqs met. Actually wear visible gear tiers.
- Goal-driven: pick the best XP/loot target for their level (rats -> goblins -> harder), level up over time, keep stats in saves.
- Sub-types: "Adventurer" (PvE, avoids the north unless strong), "Raider" (~4: PKers who go north to hunt players/AI for gear & coin), "Opportunist" (goes north for richer rewards, fights back).

## PK zone ("the Scorch")
- Covers the northern end of the continent (pick a y-line on the map export; document exact rect in data/pk_zones.json).
- Biome: charred earth tiles (dark ash ground, burnt trees/stumps, embers), pixel-art placeholders swappable via sprites.json.
- Entry warning on first crossing + visible boundary (charred edge). HUD skull/zone label.
- Rules: anyone can attack anyone (player<->AI, AI<->AI). The WHOLE Scorch is a MULTI-COMBAT zone (user confirmed t31u): add its rect to data/combat_zones.json; raiders may gang up, AI groups fight together; HUD crossed-swords icon shows there.
- Death in zone: keep 3 most valuable items; everything else drops as a pile the killer gets private first (100 ticks), then public. Coin fee as normal. Outside zone death rules unchanged.
- Raiders target weaker/richer-looking players, flee when losing; killer gets a "skulled" flag for N minutes (loses the keep-3 protection).
- Risk/reward: richer resources/enemies in the Scorch (e.g. higher-tier ore/coal, a tougher enemy camp) so going north is worth it. Data-driven.
- Economy: gear lost in PK re-enters the market via raiders selling loot.
- QA bot checks + soak (raider kill rate, adventurer gear progression curve over sim days). Regenerate world map.

## AI Scorch death = new player (user request t48u/t49u)
User verbatim: "I want to simulate new players so only scorch deaths for ai only reset"
- AI player killed inside the Scorch: that character "quits" and a NEW level-1 player replaces it (new name, new appearance tint, same persona class/home weighting randomized, all skills level 1, starter kit, ~25 marks). Population count stays constant.
- The old character's banked goods are listed on the Exchange as an estate sale (goods stay in the economy; proceeds vanish = coin sink); its carried items drop per the normal Scorch death rules (keep-3 doesn't carry over to the new character).
- AI deaths elsewhere: unchanged (no reset). Player deaths: never reset.
- Soak: report AI level distribution over 30 sim days, number of resets, tier supply/prices stability.
